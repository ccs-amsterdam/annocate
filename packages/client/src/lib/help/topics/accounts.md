Everyone who logs in to an AnnoTinder server has an account with one of
three roles. They are about the server as a whole; what someone may do in
a particular job is their role there (see [Working together](working-together.md)).

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **User** | Code, and work on jobs a researcher gave them a role in (for instance as a student assistant who edits the codebook) |
| **Researcher** | Also create jobs. The jobs you create are yours: they count against your limits |
| **Admin** | Also manage the server's users, limits and registration (see [Server admin](server-admin.md)) |

Coders don't need an account at all, unless an invitation asks them to
[log in](invitations.md#login).

## Becoming a researcher

Depending on the server, you are a researcher as soon as you log in (a
public server, or an email address at your university's domain), or you
become one:

- with an **invite link** from the server's admins: open it and log in;
- by **asking**: on the jobs page, write who you are and what you want to
  code. An admin approves the request.

## Limits

A server can limit how much a researcher stores: the number of jobs you
own, the units per job and in all your jobs, the coders per job, and the
answers per job. Your limits come from your plan: the server's default,
your university's (by email domain), or one an admin gave you. The jobs page
shows what you use of them.

Limits are checked when something is added. When a job reaches one, adding
more is refused (a unit upload, or a new coder: coders who already started
go on). Nothing is ever removed because of a limit. Ask the server's admins
if you need more.

Besides that, every server limits the size of one unit and one answer, so
a single unit can't fill the server (for example an image pasted into a
unit as text).

## Two-factor authentication

A server can require a second factor (such as an authenticator app or a
passkey) for managing jobs. Logging in without one then only lets you code.
Set the second factor up with your login provider, then log out and in
again.
