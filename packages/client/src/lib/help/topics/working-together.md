## Who has access

On the study's **Settings** page, **Who has access** lists the study's
people and roles (only admins of the study see it).

- **Add** someone by email address and role. If they never logged in, they
  are _invited_ and see the study once they log in with that address
  (within 3 months).
- Change a role with the select by their name; remove someone with the bin
  icon.
- A role can be _turned off_ for safety (an expired invitation, or someone
  back after a year without a second factor). **Restore** it only if
  you're sure the address is still theirs.
- Giving or taking admin asks you to confirm it's you (see
  [Accounts and limits](accounts.md)). A study always keeps one admin.

## Roles

| Role      | Can                                                                                                          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Read**  | see the study and progress, download results                                                                 |
| **Write** | also edit and save the design draft, preview, upload units, make sets, manage links                         |
| **Admin** | also deploy, pause, reset answers, delete coders' data, rename, export or delete the study, manage access    |

Give the smallest role that works. Coders are not on this list: they come
in through a [link](links.md).

## Editing together

Everyone with **Write** edits the same draft. If two people save at once,
the second chooses whose changes to keep (see
[Working with co-editors](design-editor.md#working-with-co-editors)).
Agree who edits when, and leave deploying to one person.

Scripts use an API key that acts as you, with your roles. See
[Scripts and the researcher API](researcher-api.md).
