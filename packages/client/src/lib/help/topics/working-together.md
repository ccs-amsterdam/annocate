A job can have several researchers: a principal investigator, colleagues,
student assistants who prepare units, a methodologist who only downloads
the results. Each has a **role** in the job.

## Who has access

On the job's **Settings** tab, **Who has access** lists the people of the job and
their roles. Only admins of the job see this list and can change it.

- **Add** someone with their email address and a role. They see the job
  once they log in to AnnoTinder with that address.
- Change a role with the select next to their name.
- Remove someone with the bin icon.

A job always keeps at least one admin, so it can't lose its last one by
accident.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Read** | See the job, its coders' progress, and download the results |
| **Write** | Also edit and save the codebook (and preview it), upload units, make unitsets and invitations |
| **Admin** | Also publish the codebook, pause the job, reset answers, delete coders' data, rename or delete the job, and manage who has access |

Give people the smallest role that lets them do their work. Coders are not
on this list: they never log in to the job's management, they come in through
an [invitation](invitations.md).

## Editing together

Everyone with **Write** works on the same codebook draft. AnnoTinder doesn't
show who else is editing right now: if two people save at once, the second
is asked whether to keep their own changes or load the other's (see
[Working with co-editors](codebook-editor.md#working-with-co-editors)).
Agree who edits when, and leave publishing to one person.

## Scripts

Scripts (R, Python) use an API key that acts as you, with your roles in your
jobs. See [Scripts and the researcher API](researcher-api.md).
