## A study in five steps {#start}
<!-- show: map -->
1. **Design**: what coders see and answer, and who codes.
2. **Units**: what they code, uploaded as a CSV.
3. **Coders**: expert coders you add, or a crowd that comes by one link.
4. **Deploy**: makes your drafts live, and makes the links coders start from.
5. **Results**: the answers, the agreement between coders, and the downloads.
