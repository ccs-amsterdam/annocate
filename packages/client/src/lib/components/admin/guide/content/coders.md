## Adding expert coders {#add-coders}
<!-- show: add-coders; show me: Coders / add-coders / Add coders: an id each, optionally an email address -->
- Expert coders are added here, one per line: a coder id (a name, a number), and optionally the email address they sign in with.
- A crowd's coders aren't added: they appear here when they start with a coder id from their link.

## Making a coder set {#coder-set}
<!-- show: coders; show me: Coders / make-coder-set / Make coder set: Select coders, Manage coders -->
- The [coder set](sets.md) says who codes which units.
- **Make coder set** has two tabs: Select coders (who gets units, and how they are divided) and Manage coders (adding coders).
- It is a draft until you deploy the units: the table shows the units assigned now and in the draft.

## Following the coders {#coders-table}
<!-- show: coders; show me: Coders / coders-table / Coders: units and progress per coder -->
- The table shows each coder's units: assigned, done, and remaining.
- A notice above it says how many units no coder has.
- Deleting a coder deletes their answers for good, and takes them out of the coder set: their units have no coder until you divide them again.
